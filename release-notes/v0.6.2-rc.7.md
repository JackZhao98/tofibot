# TOFI v0.6.2-rc.7

## Bots split work and hand parts to the crew

- When a request has independent parts, the Bot you asked can act as the lead: it plans the parts, gives each part to a group member whose role fits (or to its own sub-runs when no member fits), runs them in parallel, checks the results and gives you one answer. This also works when you @mention only that Bot.
- One visible message shows who does which part. The lead's task card is the plan: each part is a checklist item with its owner, deadline and state, and it updates when a part is done, fails or times out.
- A member's result for its part is folded under the card. Replies to you, approval cards, questions, files and errors always stay visible.
- Each part can have a result format, a deadline and limits. A part's deadline starts when its member starts work. Defaults: 30 minutes, or 60 minutes in a scheduled run. A part that is late is marked late on the card.
- Fan-out is bounded: at most 4 parts at once and 2 levels deep. Settings > Behavior has the width, the depth and both deadlines.
- A Bot can update one progress line on its task card while it works, without new messages.

## Work trace

- Screenshots can be taken at overview size (about a quarter of the cost) for state checks; full size stays the default for reading text.
- A Bot can stitch the screenshots it already took in a run into one image to check its work.
- At the end of a run that used the Bot computer, a folded "Work trace" line shows the steps as pictures. Click a step to open it. Pages from the Playwright browser are included.
- Privacy: no picture is stored while a secret is being typed and until the page changes (at most 15 minutes, for every Bot on that computer), during a takeover, or on websites in your private list. The page address is checked when the picture is taken. Settings > Behavior has Work trace on or off, how many days to keep pictures (default 7) and the private website list.

## Fixes
