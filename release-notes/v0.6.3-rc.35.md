# TOFI v0.6.3-rc.35

Steps in the new design, work moved to its own panel, and failures shown on the step that failed.

## Chat

- **Steps use the new list design.** Done steps tick, the current step is highlighted, waiting and stopped steps have their own marks.
- **Work has its own panel.** The work log opens in the 工作过程 panel on the right. The chat no longer shows the "N steps" rows. Open it from a message's menu or the hover bar.
- **A failure sits on its step.** When a task with a list fails, the step it stopped on gets a cross with the reason and the next action. Finished steps stay ticked.

## Layout

- **Narrower right column.** Widgets now take 24% of the window width, between 288 and 336 px.
- **Stronger blur behind the header cat.**
- **Sidebar drag keeps its order.** A refresh that started before you dropped a conversation no longer puts the old order back.
