# TOFI v0.6.3-rc.29

Sidebar drag moves to a standard library.

## Sidebar

- **Dragging conversations works as before, on a new engine.** You can still drag a chat to pin it, reorder pins, and drag it back to unpin it. With the keyboard, Alt and the arrow keys move a pin. On a phone, a long press starts a drag.
- **Smoother near the edges.** The list scrolls one step per frame while you drag near the top or bottom.
- **Screen readers** announce sidebar rows as buttons again.
