# TOFI v0.6.3-rc.27

A shorter path when the answer check finds nothing to change.

## Bots

- **An unchanged answer goes out as it is.** Before a Bot sends its answer, it checks the answer against its work. When the check finds nothing to change, the Bot now sends the draft it already wrote, instead of writing the whole answer again.
