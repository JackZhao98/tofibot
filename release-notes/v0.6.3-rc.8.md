# TOFI v0.6.3-rc.8

One switch connects your Mac, organization members can use their own ChatGPT sign-in, and Settings are quieter.

## New

- **Remote control this Mac** (needs the Mac app 0.2.2, built for this release). In the Mac app, Settings › Environments has one switch, "Remote control this Mac". Pairing codes are gone.
  - When you turn the switch on, macOS asks you to confirm once.
  - While the switch is on, all your Bots can use this Mac. A Bot that you denied on this Mac stays denied.
  - If a macOS permission is missing, the row names it and gives you a "Grant" button. The Mac connects as soon as one permission is granted.
  - When you turn the switch off, control stops and the Mac is removed from your environments.
  - Approval cards, the banner on screen and the ⌃⌥⌘Esc stop key work as before.
- **Your own ChatGPT sign-in next to organization models.** When your organization provides the AI models, you can also sign in to ChatGPT and use your own Codex models.
  - Administrators choose this per group or per person: "Members bring their own", "Organization provides, members may also use their own", or "Organization models only".
  - Your own Codex calls do not count toward the organization's usage.
  - A Bot that follows the default model never moves by itself between organization models and your own.

## Changed

- **Settings look quieter.** Only the window frame and at most one orange button per page keep the bold style. Other controls are plain filled blocks. The side list has fewer headings and no account card. Page headers are shorter, and the content column is narrower and easier to read. Status shows as a dot and a word. Pages, settings and links are the same as before.
