# TOFI v0.6.3-rc.42

The shell, sign-in, the Mac app and covers move onto the new design.

## Devices

- **Switching shows its step.** Starting, packing, copying (with MB), unpacking.
- **Shorter copy.** No parenthetical explanations on devices and the Bot computer.
- **Caches stay behind.** A switch or image update skips `.npm` and `.cache`.
- **Pages reload after an update.** An open page loads the new version instead of a dead button.

## Shell

- **Fixed widget order.** Bot, computer, terminal, artifacts. Spend shows as a banner from 80% of the limit.
- **Terminal** fills its widget and follows the theme.
- **Covers** use the new dialog; a Bot-to-Bot chat opens as a read-only window.
- **Bot settings** pick the avatar colour from swatches.
- **Bot computer** has a new driver switch in the large window.

## Sign-in and first run

- **Sign-in and owner setup** use the new cards and fields.
- **Empty states** show still art instead of animated cats.
- **Search** uses the new dialog and tabs.

## Mac app and other pages

- **Mac sidebar** shows the system material behind it.
- **Mac pages and the Bot browser toolbar** use the new controls.
- **OAuth completion page** uses the new card and the calico logo.
