# TOFI v0.6.3-rc.11

A calmer, simpler look, and a Bot's work you can follow step by step.

## New

- **See what a Bot is doing.** While a Bot works, its steps fold into short lines such as "Ran 3 commands"; between them the Bot says in one sentence what it found. Click a line to see its steps.
- **Step pictures.** The pictures a Bot takes while it works sit as a small stack next to their step. Click the stack to look at them large on a dark background; use the arrow keys or swipe to move between them.
- **Update button in the Mac app** (needs Mac app 0.2.5). When an update is ready, a small download icon appears next to Settings at the bottom left. Click it to restart and update.

## Changed

- **A quieter look.** Thin lines and soft shadows replace the thick outlines, in light and dark mode. Menus, panels and dialogs are frosted and fade in place.
- **Dark mode** has no white line between the sidebar and the chat, no light shadow at the bottom, and closer greys.
- **Files and pictures from a Bot** appear below its reply, not inside the bubble, and smaller.
- **The account menu** has Settings, Usage, Artifacts and Language. The theme is one icon. Report a problem, feedback, security and GitHub are in Settings › General.
- **Bottom left** shows your name only. On a self-hosted server it also shows the server's address.
- **Settings › AI models** shows one row per provider; a key or address form opens only when you add that provider.
- **Mac app with the sidebar collapsed:** the sidebar buttons and the chat title share the row with the window buttons.
- **The version card** is in Administration on a server with several accounts.

## Fixes

- **Mac app 0.2.5:** the Tofi menu bar and Dock icon no longer disappear when the Bot computer mini window is open.
- **Usage and Memory panels** open in the Mac app, and Escape closes them also while they load.
- **In the Mac app**, the window can be dragged by its top strip while a side panel is open.
