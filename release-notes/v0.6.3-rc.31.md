# TOFI v0.6.3-rc.31

The Bot's cat is now the chat header, and it opens a Bot card beside the chat.

## Chat

- **One large cat in the header.** A chat with a Bot shows its cat at the top, without the name. The messages blur softly near the cat as they scroll under it.
- **Tap the cat to open the Bot card.** The cat moves to the top of the right column, and a card opens under it. You can change the Bot's name and role there, and see what it is doing now.
- **More in the card.** The menu button shows the Bot computer, memory and scheduled tasks. "Advanced settings" opens Bot settings, and a tap on the card's cat opens the avatar settings.

## Avatars

- **The header cat keeps its colours** when the Bot is idle. Before, it looked grey.
- **The cat is centred** in its tile in Bot settings.
- **No tile in group chats.** Message heads and member lists show only the cat.

## Scrolling

- **Content no longer shifts when a scrollbar appears.** Every scrolling area keeps room for its scrollbar.
