# Trademark policy

This page says how you can use the TOFI name and logo. It is not legal advice.

The name "TOFI" and the calico cat logo identify the official TOFI project
(<https://github.com/JackZhao98/tofibot>). The logo files are in
[ui/public/brand/](ui/public/brand/).

## You may

- Say that your product is "based on TOFI" or "a fork of TOFI".
- Use the name TOFI to refer to this project, for example in an article, a
  tutorial or a comparison.
- Show unmodified screenshots of TOFI in articles and reviews.

## You may not, without written permission

- Use TOFI or the logo as the name or logo of your product, fork, service,
  company or domain name.
- Say or imply that the TOFI project made, approved or endorsed your product.
- Change the logo, or make a logo that looks like it.

If you publish a fork, rename it and use your own logo.
