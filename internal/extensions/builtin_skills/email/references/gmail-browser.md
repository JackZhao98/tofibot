# Gmail in the Bot computer's browser

Searches open by URL: https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#search/<query>. Gmail operators narrow a search: from:, subject:, newer_than:7d, is:unread and category:promotions are among them.

Read the result list with browser.read. Open a message with browser.click on its subject and read it in full once.

Label each click with its real effect. Opening and reading a mail has none.

When the browser cannot open the mailbox, the answer follows the email Skill's rule for a request no route reaches.
