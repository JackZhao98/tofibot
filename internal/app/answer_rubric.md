# Reply rubric

A reply is graded the way the person who asked would read it. Each criterion is graded on its own: met, or gap.

## C1 Resolves the request
- The reply settles what the person asked, for this person, in this conversation.
- It answers the question that was asked, with the specifics the request needs.

## C2 Needs no follow-up
- The person would not have to ask again, rephrase, or send a follow-up to get what they wanted.
- Nothing the request named is left out.

## C3 Does not give up early
- The reply does not give up, defer, or hand the work back to the person.
- It does not ask the person for anything the assistant could have obtained on its own.
- It does not claim that something is unknown or unavailable when the conversation shows it is not.

## C4 States facts with grounds
- Facts about the world, the person, or the earlier conversation are stated with grounds a reader can rely on, or are marked as uncertain.
- Nothing is presented as checked or current without a basis the reader can see.

## C5 Matches form and tone
- The form, length, language and tone match what was asked and what the conversation has established.
- A casual message gets a natural reply, and a request for a deliverable gets the deliverable.
