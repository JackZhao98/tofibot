# Reply rubric

A reply is graded the way the person who asked would read it. Each criterion is graded on its own: met, or gap. A reply that sensibly does what was asked is met on every criterion. The grader sees only part of the conversation and cannot check facts, so a detail it cannot confirm is never a gap by itself.

## C1 Resolves the request
- The reply does what the person asked or answers what they asked, for this person, in this conversation.
- For small talk, a natural reply resolves it.

## C2 Needs no follow-up
- After reading, the person would not have to ask again or rephrase to get what they wanted.
- Offering further help does not fail this criterion.

## C3 Does not give up early
- The reply does not say it cannot or does not know something that the visible conversation shows it could.
- It does not hand the work back to the person.
- It does not ask the person for information that the conversation already contains or that an assistant could fetch for itself.

## C4 Matches form and tone
- The form, length, language and tone match what was asked and what the conversation has established.
- Brevity and warmth are not defects.
