# TOFI v0.6.3-rc.21

The step list follows the work: done means done, the list changes when you write again, and steps show their pictures.

## Step list

- **Done means the work happened.** A step becomes done only after the Bot finished work for it. A step marked done without work stays open. The Bot's answer finishes the step in progress. Steps that the answer did not reach show as skipped in the run's history.
- **The list follows what you write.** When you send a message while a Bot works, the Bot re-assesses its steps. It can add, drop or re-order them, or start a new list. A follow-up request can continue the earlier list with its done steps.
- **Pictures in steps.** A step that made or used a picture (a screenshot, a generated or published image, an image the Bot looked at) shows a small stack of thumbnails after the step text. Click it to open the newest picture.
