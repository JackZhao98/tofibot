# TOFI v0.6.3-rc.22

Bots send what you ask to see, and they can watch and hear a video.

## Bots

- **Bots send the thing, not a link.** When you ask to see or to have something, the Bot sends it in its message: a picture from a web page, a file on the Bot computer, a screen capture, a generated image or a file of the conversation. The Bot's message tool is now called `send_message` and carries up to 8 attachments. Old conversations show as before.
- **Videos.** A Bot can watch a video that you send or that is on its computer. It samples frames, makes a contact sheet, reads the text on the screen and tells you what happens when. The procedure is the built-in "video" Skill, which the Bot reads only when it needs it.
- **Speech in audio and video.** The new `transcribe_audio` tool turns the speech in a file into text. It uses the same transcription service as dictation, so it needs a Codex sign-in, an OpenAI key or a transcription key.
- **Mac browser on pages with embedded frames.** With the next Mac app, reading a page in the Mac app's Bot browser also works on pages with ads, videos or other embedded frames, and the Bot is told how many frames it could not read.
