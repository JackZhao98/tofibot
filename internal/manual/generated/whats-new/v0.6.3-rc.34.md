# TOFI v0.6.3-rc.34

Chat scrolling fixes, a springier header cat, and more Settings pages on the new design kit.

## Chat

- **The chat scrolls while a widget is open.** With the Bot card, the computer or another widget in the right column, the wheel over the messages scrolled nothing. It scrolls the messages again.
- **A refresh keeps your place.** After a page refresh the chat returns to where you were reading. A chat with no saved place opens at the newest message.
- **The header cat springs.** It lands on the Bot card with a small bounce and springs back to the header when the card closes.
- **No jump when you open a Bot chat.** The message list no longer moves a few pixels after it appears.

## Settings

- **Administration resources, backups, file move and storage** use the new buttons.
- **Button labels stay on one line** in Settings rows and key forms.
