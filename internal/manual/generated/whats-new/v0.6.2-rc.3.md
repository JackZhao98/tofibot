# TOFI v0.6.2-rc.3

## Bots

- In a group, a Bot that leaves its part as a note for another Bot and ends without a reply now shows a collapsed "Left a note" row. Before, its note was hidden, and the next Bot's reply could refer to text that you could not see.
- OpenAI models use OpenAI's own web search before the computer. A search key or SearXNG that you set still comes first. Before, a Bot with a computer searched on the computer, and the search failed when the computer could not start.

## Computer

- After an update, a hibernated computer stays hibernated until it is used, then starts on the new release. Before, the update started every account's computer at once; they used CPU and memory for 15 minutes and could keep another account's computer from starting.
