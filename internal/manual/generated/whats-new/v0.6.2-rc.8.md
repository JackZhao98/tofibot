# TOFI v0.6.2-rc.8

Fixes from live testing of rc.7.

## Divide and conquer

- When the Bot you asked splits a request, it now gets one plan card right after its assignment message: your request as the title, each part as a checklist item with its owner and state. Before, the parts showed as separate cards above the assignment.
- Each member's result folds into its item on the plan card. Before, the full results showed as normal messages. Reactions and delivery marks stay on a message when it folds.
- If the lead Bot stops with an error, the parts keep going and the lead continues when they are done. If you stop the lead, its parts stop too.
- The plan card closes when the lead gives its answer, or shows as blocked when a part is still open.

## Work trace

- In a chat where Activity is hidden, the "Work trace" line now shows under the Bot's reply, so you can open the pictures.

## Fixes

- Replies that used OpenAI's web search no longer show raw citation codes such as "cite turn784583view0". Older messages hide them too.
