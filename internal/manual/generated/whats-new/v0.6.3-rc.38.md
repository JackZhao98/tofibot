# TOFI v0.6.3-rc.38

The Version card is back in System, and the idle E2B computer can be upgraded again.

## Settings

- **Version is in System.** On a server with several accounts, the Version card (installed version, check for updates, update) was at the bottom of Accounts. Administrators find it in System again.
- **Upgrade on the idle E2B computer.** When E2B sits beside the built-in VM and has a newer image, its line shows Upgrade again, and the upgrade works.
- **Attach stays available.** An administrator can attach the built-in VM to an account that already lists another computer.
