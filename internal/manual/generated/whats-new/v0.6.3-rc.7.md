# TOFI v0.6.3-rc.7

Notifications now come from the server, and the Mac app can show them when its window is closed.

## New

- **The server decides what to notify you about.** Before, each browser tab decided by itself. Now the server records the events that need you and every client reads the same list. In a browser, notifications look the same as before, and you get none for the conversation you are reading.
- **Two new kinds of notification**, both on by default in Settings › General: "Questions waiting for you" (a Bot stops and waits for your answer or approval) and "Spend limit alerts" (you reach 80% of the monthly limit, or the limit itself). An approval that the automatic reviewer decides by itself does not notify you.
- **Mac app notifications** (needs the Mac app version built for this release). The app shows system notifications also when its window is closed. Click a notification to open the conversation. In Settings › General › This device you can turn notifications off on this Mac, hide the message content, and open Tofi at login (off by default). If your server is too old to send notifications, the app says so.

## Fixes

- When no model is connected, the message box no longer lets the messages behind it show through, and the cat no longer sits on top of a message.
