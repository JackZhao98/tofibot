# TOFI v0.6.3-rc.24

A clearer Remove for an earlier storage bucket.

## File storage

- **Remove says why it waits.** After you change the bucket, the earlier bucket can be removed only after the deletion grace period. The Remove button stays disabled until then and shows the date when it becomes available. A refused Remove names the real reason.
