# TOFI v0.6.3-rc.5

Fixes for the Mac app header and the Settings window.

## Fixes

- In the Mac app, the Bot name and every button on the right of the conversation header respond again. In v0.6.3-rc.4 the strip that moves the window covered them. The empty part of the header still moves the window.
- In the Mac app, opening Settings no longer makes the conversation narrower.
- The Settings window is smaller. It takes at most 88% of the width and 86% of the height of the window, so it stays clear of the window controls in the Mac app. Other pop-up windows use the same sizes.
- Settings › AI Models shows its cards in one column, so the Dictation card has room for its full text.
