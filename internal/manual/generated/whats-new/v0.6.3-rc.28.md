# TOFI v0.6.3-rc.28

A first look at the new design. Some screens still have the old look; they change in the next releases.

## New look

- **New colours and type.** The whole app uses the new palette and fonts, in light and dark.
- **One style for controls.** Buttons, fields, dropdowns, switches and segmented choices look and behave the same everywhere. On a phone, every control is at least 44 px tall to tap.
- **Menus, dialogs, toasts and hints** share one style. A confirmation now asks in a dialog, with Cancel selected.

## Chat

- **A new frame for the conversation.** Messages sit on a dot grid, with a soft blur at the top and the bottom. A round arrow takes you back to the latest message.
- **The header is the Bot's cat.** It is large at the top of the chat and small when you scroll. The header has at most three buttons: Tasks and Members are in More, and one computer button opens the Bot computer or a browser on your Mac.

## Sidebar

- **One row style for conversations and pins**, with the unread count, waiting and failed marks, and a working mark on group chats too. The account and Settings have their own rows at the bottom.

## Settings

- **Settings is a full page.** Its sections are grouped as You, Organization and Server. On a phone, the sections are one row of links.
- **General is rebuilt.** Notifications fold into one row that tells you when something needs attention, such as a blocked browser permission. You can copy the server address.
