# TOFI v0.6.3-rc.10

A Bot browser on your Mac that does not take your keyboard or mouse, a live mini window for it, and uploaded files that expire.

## New

- **A Bot browser on your Mac** (needs Mac app 0.2.4, built for this release; install it by hand once if you have 0.2.2).
  - When you ask a Bot to use your Mac's browser, it works in its own browser window in the Mac app. It drives the page directly and does not move your pointer or type into your apps. You can keep working while it works.
  - The Bot browser has its own sign-ins, separate from your everyday browser. Sign in there once and it stays signed in.
  - Click into the Bot browser to take over; the Bot waits until you click "Return to Bot".
  - Mac app settings: "Allow Bots to use the dedicated browser" (on, under Advanced), "Keep browser sign-ins" (on, with Clear now) and "Allow window previews through the server" (on).
- **A live mini window for the Bot browser.** The mini window shows "Your Mac · Bot browser" as continuous video, also when you have no Bot computer. In the Mac app you can drag it outside the window.
- **Uploaded files expire.** On a cloud server, files you upload and working files that a Bot makes are deleted after 30 days. A self-hosted server keeps them. The period is a setting. An expired file shows "Expired" in the chat. A Bot can keep a file it needs later; the card then shows which Bot kept it.

## Changed

- **Only results count as Artifacts.** A screenshot of your Mac shared into the chat and a mail-draft attachment copied from a Bot computer are now Files, not Artifacts. Storage shows them as "Bot files". This applies to new files only.

## Fixes

- **Settings no longer freeze** on Administration › Organization resources. A card that was still loading blocked every click, including Close.
- **Mac app 0.2.4:** the Tofi icon no longer disappears from the Dock while a Bot controls the Mac, and the enlarged mini window's Close and Shrink buttons can be clicked.
