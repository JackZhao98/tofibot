# TOFI v0.6.3-rc.30

No flash when you drop a conversation in the sidebar.

## Sidebar

- **A pin or an unpin lands smoothly.** The row stays full size and keeps its cat while it settles into its new place.
- **The rows around it glide.** When you unpin a chat, the pins under it slide up. Rows that come into view slide in with the rest.
