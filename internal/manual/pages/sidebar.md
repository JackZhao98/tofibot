# The sidebar, pins and views

Open this page: [{{ui:chat:sidebar.pinned}}](/)

The sidebar lists Bots and groups. The list is ordered by recent activity until a person drags a chat to another place. Then the person's order wins.

- **Pin a chat.** A pinned chat sits in the pinned area at the top. Pin from the chat's menu, or ask a Bot to pin it with the settings tool (`conversation.pinned`). An archived chat cannot be pinned.
- **Search.** The magnifier button, or Cmd or Ctrl and K, opens the search palette for chats and messages.
- **Views.** A view opens beside the chat: Tasks, Memory, Terminal and Archive. An address with `?side=tasks`, `?side=memory`, `?side=terminal` or `?side=archive` opens one.
- **Widgets.** Small blocks inside the chat: Computer, Members, Artifacts and Usage. A person folds, closes and reorders them.

The sidebar layout is stored on the server. It follows the person to every device.
