# Groups

Open this page: [{{ui:chat:members.members}}](/g/{group}/settings#members)

A group is a chat with two to eight Bots. A message that names a Bot goes to that Bot. A message that names no one is routed to the member whose role fits best, or to the Primary Assistant.

- **Name.** A group can be renamed. A direct message is named after its Bot.
- **Members.** Changing members needs the person's approval when a Bot asks for it. A member that leaves ends its open tasks in the group. A group with queued or running work cannot change members until the work ends.
- **Memory.** A group has a shared memory that every member can read.
- **Archive and delete.** An archived group is read only. Deleting a group removes its history, memory, tasks and schedules. The member Bots stay. Both need the person's approval when a Bot asks.
