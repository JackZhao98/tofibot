# connection_not_ready

Open this page: [Where to look](/settings/connections)

**Meaning.** The sign-in finished, but the server does not see the token yet.

**Usual cause.** The sign-in did not complete, or the service denied it.

**Steps.**
1. Open the connection and sign in again.
2. Check the status shows Connected.
