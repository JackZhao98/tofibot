# settings_target_not_found

Open this page: [Where to look](/settings/general)

**Meaning.** The Bot, conversation or connection does not exist in this workspace.

**Usual cause.** The ID is wrong, or the target was deleted.

**Steps.**
1. Read ids with list, or from the workspace.
2. Call again with the right id.
