# work_trace_off

Open this page: [{{ui:settings:shell.page.advanced.name}}](/settings/advanced#computer)

**Meaning.** The action desktop.trace needs stored captures, and this workspace keeps none.

**Usual cause.** The setting {{ui:settings:automation.item.work_trace.name}} is off.

**Steps.**
1. Use desktop.capture to look at the screen.
2. To keep captures, a person turns the setting on in Settings.
