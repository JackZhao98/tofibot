# mcp_permission_denied

Open this page: [Where to look](/settings/connections)

**Meaning.** The service refused the connection's access for this call.

**Usual cause.** The sign-in of the service lacks a scope, or the account lacks a right.

**Steps.**
1. Tell the person which tool was refused.
2. The person checks the sign-in on the page of the connection.
