# needs_observation

Open this page: [Where to look](/)

**Meaning.** The Bot computer needs a fresh look at the screen before this action.

**Usual cause.** The screen changed since the last capture or snapshot.

**Steps.**
1. Take a snapshot or a capture.
2. Send the action again with a target from the new view.
