# mcp_review_policy_denied

Open this page: [Where to look](/settings/connections)

**Meaning.** The review refused the call. The call was not sent.

**Usual cause.** The call goes beyond what the user asked, or it sends data to a recipient that the user did not name.

**Steps.**
1. Read the reason in the message.
2. Change the call to match the request, or ask the person.
3. Do not route around the refusal.
