# settings_change_not_revertable

Open this page: [Where to look](/settings/advanced#changes)

**Meaning.** This change cannot be undone with the tool.

**Usual cause.** The value was too long to keep, the change is an action, or it was itself an undo.

**Steps.**
1. The person can change the setting by hand on its page.
