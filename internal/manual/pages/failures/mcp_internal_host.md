# mcp_internal_host

Open this page: [Where to look](/settings/connections)

**Meaning.** A Bot cannot add a connection on a private or local address.

**Usual cause.** The address is on a local network, loopback or another internal range.

**Steps.**
1. The person adds such a connection on the Connections page.
