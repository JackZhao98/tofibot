# model_auth_invalid

Open this page: [Where to look](/settings/models#providers)

**Meaning.** The model provider refused the key or the sign-in.

**Usual cause.** The key was revoked, expired or mistyped, or the sign-in ended.

**Steps.**
1. Open Models and connect the provider again.
