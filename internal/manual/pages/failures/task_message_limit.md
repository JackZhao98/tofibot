# task_message_limit

Open this page: [Where to look](/settings/automation#reliability)

**Meaning.** The Bots passed the limit of messages to each other without progress.

**Usual cause.** A loop of messages with no result.

**Steps.**
1. Report progress or a result, or ask the person.
2. The count resets when a task makes progress.
