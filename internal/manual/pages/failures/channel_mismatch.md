# channel_mismatch

Open this page: [Where to look](/settings/advanced#version)

**Meaning.** The version is not on the chosen channel.

**Usual cause.** A release candidate was asked for on the stable channel, or the reverse.

**Steps.**
1. Switch the channel on the Version card, or choose another version.
