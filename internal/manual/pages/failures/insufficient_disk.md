# insufficient_disk

Open this page: [Where to look](/settings/advanced#version)

**Meaning.** There is not enough free disk space for the backup that comes before an update.

**Usual cause.** The disk is nearly full.

**Steps.**
1. Free disk space on the server.
2. Try again.
