# takeover_active

Open this page: [Where to look](/)

**Meaning.** A capture was refused because the person has the screen for a step.

**Usual cause.** A takeover card of the run is open. The person's screen is not shown to a Bot and is not kept in the work trace.

**Steps.**
1. Wait for the handover to end.
2. Capture the screen again and continue from the page you see.
