# settings_forbidden

Open this page: [Where to look](/settings/general)

**Meaning.** This setting is not changed by a Bot. The person changes it on its page.

**Usual cause.** The key holds a secret, an account, the update, data export or purge, Bot computer resources, sign-out, or the cap on the settings tool.

**Steps.**
1. Give the person the link to the page.
2. Do not look for another way to change it.
